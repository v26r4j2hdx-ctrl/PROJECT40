Fine rules

The overdue penalty is divided into two tiers.

First 7 days ₹2 per overdue day
After 7 days ₹5 per additional overdue day
Maximum threshold ₹1,000
Example

10 overdue days = 7 × ₹2 + 3 × ₹5 = ₹29
